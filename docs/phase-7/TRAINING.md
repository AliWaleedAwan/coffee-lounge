# Training sheets, one per role

Each sheet fits on one printed page. Names in **bold** are what you see on screen. Before the visit (I-25) the owner checks that each sheet matches how the shop works.

---

## Waiter (tablet or own phone)

**Start:** open the shop address; tap your name on **Who is using this device?** and type your PIN. Tap **Switch user** when you hand the device over; it locks itself when idle.

1. **Floor**: your tables. A table with a guest order waiting shows it; tap the table to open it.
2. **Approvals**: orders guests sent from their phones. Check the table, then accept. Rejecting needs a reason (for example "Item just ran out").
3. Ordering for a guest: open the table → **Add item** → choose size and options → **Send to kitchen**.
4. **Pickup**: when you take a drink or dish from the counter to the table, tap **Picked up …** on it. Tapped by mistake: **Undo** within a few seconds.
5. Cancel or void a line: on the line, give the reason; "Was it already made?" decides if the stock is used. A void after pickup needs a manager.
6. The guest asks for the bill: **Request bill**. The cashier takes payment.

If the screen says **Reconnecting…**: wait; nothing is lost. Do not press twice.

---

## Cashier (counter iMac)

**Start of day:** **Bills & payments** shows "No cash shift is open": type the float you counted in **Opening float (Rs)** → **Open shift**. While a shift is open, the bar at the top shows the cash in the till and links to **Cash shift**.

1. **Bills & payments**: bills waiting. Open one, check it with the guest, lock it, then take payment: **Cash** (type what was handed over; the change is shown), **Card** (approval code optional), or a transfer (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank: the transaction ID is required).
2. Split payment: take part by one method, then the rest by another.
3. Discount: within your limit directly; above it, a manager approves on your screen with their PIN.
4. Takeaway at the counter: **New takeaway** → items → pay. After a payment, **Next customer** clears the screen for the next one.
5. Refunds: **Refunds & corrections** → find the bill → amount and reason → the owner (or a refund approver) types their PIN. A card number is never typed anywhere.
6. Paid in / paid out (milk top-up, change from the bank): **Cash shift** → the movement with its reason. The till never pays out more than it should hold.
7. **End of day:** count the drawer, **Cash shift** (in the bar at the top) → close with the counted amount. A difference needs a reason; a manager approves it.

Deliveries: **Deliveries**: dispatch to the rider, record the return (cash handed in, or failed with the reason).

---

## Rider handover (cashier records it)

Riders have no login (D-41). The cashier records **dispatch** (rider, cash to collect) and the **return** (cash received, or why the delivery failed). A failed prepaid delivery shows a refund due.

---

## Inventory clerk (counter iMac, off-peak, D-38)

1. **Inventory** → **Receive stock**: scan or pick the item, quantity, pack, cost; lot and expiry where asked. Post the receipt. A mistake: **Reverse this receipt…** (only if nothing has used it yet), or a supplier return.
2. **Inventory** → **Waste**: item, quantity, reason (spilled, expired …).
3. **Inventory** → **Counts** → **Start count** (blind: you do not see the book quantity) → type what you counted → **Submit count**. A difference above the threshold waits for a manager.
4. **Inventory** → **Import**: the weekly templates (items, packs, opening stock, recipes); **Preview** shows every problem before **Import**.
5. **Menu & recipes** → **Recipes**: the ingredients of each size; **Track stock with this recipe** switches stock control on for it.

## Manager

Everything a waiter and a cashier does, plus:

- Approvals above staff limits (discounts, voids after pickup, cash shift variances, count differences) with your PIN on the staff member's screen.
- **Live operations**: what is waiting, being prepared, unpaid, and the day so far.
- **Reports** (as permitted), **Devices** (register a tablet), **Settings** → **Printers** (test print, a printer off).
- Printer trouble: RUNBOOKS.md §3.
